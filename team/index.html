// Our Faculty Advisor!

Tyrone Stewart

Academic Program Manager

"I hope ComputeX will be a student-inspired, student-led organization supporting the many ways we engage with computing. As technology shapes our lives, technical understanding, communication, and social connection are essential to being informed citizens and thoughtful creators. By collaborating with other student organizations and building on their strengths, we aim to create welcoming spaces where students can explore different tools, languages, and fields without being confined to a single career path. Ultimately, I hope ComputeX can be a shared resource, toolkit, workshop, and incubator where students both contribute and discover—while developing the technical acumen, judgment and confidence to guide what comes next for self and society."
