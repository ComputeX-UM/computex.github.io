// 01. WEEKLY_CADENCE

How ComputeX Operates

Core Weekly Slot

Mondays @ 7:30 PM – 8:30 PM

Starting September 14, 2026

Our anchor weekly gathering. We alternate every week between conceptual discussion workshops (Exploratory) and hands-on coding sessions (Applied) across our 5 interdisciplinary pillars.

📍 In-person location announced via Discord & Email.
Semester Kickoff

Two Identical Mass Meetings

Early September 2026

We host two identical in-person mass meetings during onboarding week so everyone can attend regardless of class schedules. Both sessions are livestreamed and recorded for remote members.

📹 In-person + Hybrid livestreaming available.
FORMAT A //

Exploratory Workshops

Deep dives into system architecture, math foundations, ethics, and open-floor discussions. No laptop or dev setup required—sit back, join the debate, and analyze big concepts.

FORMAT B //

Applied Workshops

Hands-on implementation focusing on syntax, APIs, data pipelines, and tool deployment. Bring your laptop (VS Code/Python/Git) and leave with a working project artifact.
