Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need advanced coding experience to join as a student? +

No! We explicitly assemble interdisciplinary teams. Domain specialists in finance, biology, design, or philosophy work alongside technical software leads to ensure solutions have genuine real-world impact.

What is the weekly time commitment for fellows? +

Expect around 3 to 5 hours per week, including team standups, project work, and optional guest mentor workshops.

How can companies submit custom project challenges? +

Select "Industry / Research Partner" in the application form above or email our partnerships team directly to discuss scope and dataset sharing.
