// QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked.

GENERAL
Who can participate in ComputeX? +
Any University of Michigan student can participate, including graduate students. ComputeX is open to students across majors and skill levels.
Do I need to know how to code? +
Nope. ComputeX is about exploring how computing connects with different fields, not just about becoming a programmer.
Do I have to become a Core Member? +
Absolutely not. General members can come to any events they want, whenever they want. Core is for students who want to become more involved in shaping ComputeX.
CORE
How do I become a Core Member? +
Fill out the Core Interest Form early in the semester and stay engaged throughout the semester. As a guideline, we're looking for participation in 3 of 5 socials and at least 2 workshops, with 3 workshops preferred. At the end of the semester, anyone being considered for Core will have a conversation with the Board.
Why are socials specifically 3 of 5? +
Socials are one of the clearest ways to build community and get to know other members. Since other event types may be more tentative while ComputeX is getting started, the social requirement gives us a consistent way to look for engagement.
What happens at the end-of-semester conversation? +
It's a relatively casual conversation with the Board. We'll talk about your semester, your interests, your involvement, and whether Core makes sense for you going forward.
What can Core Members actually do? +
Core Members attend biweekly meetings with the Board and other Core Members, help shape ComputeX's direction, pitch ideas, and can design and lead workshops. There may also be access to limited-seat company visits later in the semester.
Does becoming Core guarantee a Board position? +
No. Core membership makes you eligible to pursue a Board position. Board applications are expected to open toward the end of Winter semester.
EVENTS
Can I come to an event without formally joining? +
Yes. General members can come and go as they please. You do not need to become Core or make a semester-long commitment to attend ComputeX events.
Can I suggest or lead a workshop? +
Core Members can design and lead workshops. If you have an idea before becoming Core, we'd still love to hear it.
COFFEE CHATS
What is a Coffee Chat? +
A casual 30–60 minute conversation with a ComputeX Board member. You can talk about careers, research, technology, your major, ComputeX, an idea you're working on, or just get to know someone.
Do I have to pay for coffee? +
Nope. Coffee is on us.
Can Coffee Chats be virtual? +
Yes. Coffee Chats can happen either in person or virtually.
