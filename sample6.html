Editorial Opening — Section 001

Dissolving the arbitrary boundaries between software engineering and the liberal arts, medical research, and economic policy.

ComputeX operates as an open academic sanctuary at the University of Michigan. We hold that programming is not merely a vocational skill, but a fundamentally human medium of critical thought, artistic expression, and analytical rigor.

With zero coding prerequisites, our community welcomes scholars from the College of Literature, Science, and the Arts, the College of Engineering, Ross School of Business, Stamps School of Art & Design, and the School of Information.

Supported through academic partnership with the Program in Computing for the Arts and Sciences (PCAS), we provide the practical framework necessary to turn theoretical inquiry into computational realities.
